title: "Payer quickstart"
description: "Someone sent you a PayTrace link. Here's exactly how to pay it safely."

---

You do **not** need a PayTrace account. You need:

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="A wallet" icon="wallet">
    A browser extension (MetaMask, Rabby…) or a wallet app's built-in browser.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Test USDC" icon="circle-dollar-sign">
    At least the requested amount, on Monad Testnet.
  </Card>
  <Card title="A little test MON" icon="fuel">
    To pay the network fee (gas).
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Warning>
You must pay from the **expected payer** address shown on the checkout. A payment from any other wallet will **not** count, even if the amount is correct.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the link">
    Open the `/track/...` link in a browser where your wallet is installed. On mobile, open it inside your wallet app's browser. Check the description, amount, **Receiving wallet** and **Expected payer**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Pay with your wallet">
    PayTrace calls `eth_requestAccounts`. If the selected account isn't the expected payer, it stops with a message and **nothing is sent**.

    <Accordion title="In plain English" icon="lightbulb">
    Your wallet asks which account to use. Pick the one that matches "Expected payer." If you pick the wrong one, PayTrace stops before anything happens.
    </Accordion>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve the network switch (if asked)">
    If your wallet isn't on Monad Testnet, PayTrace requests `wallet_switchEthereumChain` to `0x279f`, and if your wallet doesn't know the network (error `4902`), it requests `wallet_addEthereumChain` with the RPC and explorer pre-filled.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve the exact transfer">
    Your wallet shows a call to the USDC contract `0x534b…43A3` transferring the exact amount to the receiving wallet. Value is `0` MON (only gas is spent). There is **no approval/allowance** step.

    <Accordion title="In plain English" icon="lightbulb">
    Your wallet shows "send X USDC to [merchant address]." Check it matches the page, then confirm. You only ever approve this one exact payment.
    </Accordion>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for confirmation">
    The page shows **Confirming onchain…** and checks every 8 seconds for up to 4 minutes. Your transaction hash is saved in this browser, so closing the tab is safe: reopen the same link to resume.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get your receipt">
    When it says **All accounted for**, click **Download receipt** (JSON) or **Print receipt**, or follow **View the onchain receipt** to the block explorer.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
**Already paid from your wallet directly?** Don't pay again. Use **Already sent it?** on the checkout and paste your transaction hash. See [Verify an existing transfer](/guides/verify-existing-transfer).
</Tip>

<Note>
If the checkout says **This request is closed**, the merchant cancelled it. Do not send funds. Cancellation does not stop a transfer you send anyway, and PayTrace cannot refund it.
</Note>
