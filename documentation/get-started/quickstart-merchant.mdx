This quickstart uses the hosted release at [paytrace-tau.vercel.app](https://paytrace-tau.vercel.app). You need an Ethereum-compatible browser wallet (MetaMask, Rabby, Coinbase Wallet extension, or a wallet's in-app browser).

<Info>
**What you need before you start**
- A wallet you control, to **receive** (your merchant wallet).
- A **different** wallet that will **pay** (your customer, or a second account of yours for testing) holding test USDC and a little test MON. See [Get test tokens](/guides/get-test-tokens).
</Info>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Prepare a request without signing in" icon="file-pen">
    Open [`/start`](https://paytrace-tau.vercel.app/start). Fill in:
    - **Customer / reference**: e.g. `Acme Studio` (1–80 characters, private to you)
    - **Description**: e.g. `Website design, October` (1–240 characters, shown to the payer)
    - **Amount**: e.g. `1` or `12.500000` (positive, up to 6 decimals, up to 12 integer digits)
    - **Receiving wallet**: your merchant address
    - **Expected payer**: the customer's address (must differ from receiving wallet)

    Click to review. The draft is validated client-side and stored in this tab's `sessionStorage` under `paytrace-request-draft-v1`.

    <Accordion title="In plain English" icon="lightbulb">
    You fill in a simple form: who's paying, what it's for, how much, where the money goes and which wallet it'll come from. Nothing is saved to PayTrace yet; the draft just lives in your browser tab.
    </Accordion>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in with your wallet" icon="key-round">
    Click publish. Because you are not signed in, PayTrace redirects to `/login?next=start`. Click **Continue with wallet**, choose your account, and approve the **Sign-In with Ethereum** message. It costs nothing, moves no tokens and grants no permissions. The first sign-in creates your workspace; later sign-ins with the same wallet reopen it.

    <Accordion title="In plain English" icon="lightbulb">
    Instead of a username and password, you prove you own your wallet by "signing" a short message, like signing your name. It's free. Your wallet *is* your login, so keep access to it.
    </Accordion>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and publish" icon="send">
    You return to `/start` with the draft restored. Confirm the details and publish. The server reads the current Monad block number, stores it as the request's `afterBlock` checkpoint, assigns an ID `PT-<uuid>` and a 48-hex-character checkout token, and redirects you to `/workspace`.

    <Accordion title="In plain English" icon="lightbulb">
    PayTrace saves your request and notes the current blockchain "page number." Any payment from *before* this moment can never count for this request, which stops someone from reusing an old payment.
    </Accordion>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share the checkout link" icon="share-2">
    In the workspace, open the request and click **Open customer checkout**. Copy the URL (`/track/<token>`) and send it to your payer by any channel. Anyone with the link can see the description, amount and both wallet addresses, but never your customer name or internal notes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The payer pays" icon="wallet">
    The payer opens the link in a wallet-enabled browser and clicks **Pay with your wallet**. Their wallet switches to Monad Testnet if needed and asks them to approve an exact USDC transfer. See [Payer quickstart](/get-started/quickstart-payer).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch it become Received" icon="circle-check">
    The checkout polls verification every 8 seconds (up to 4 minutes). Once the transfer is finalized and matches every rule, the request becomes **Received** in both the checkout and your workspace (which refreshes every 15 seconds).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep the evidence" icon="download">
    Open the request to **Download evidence** (JSON) or click **Export CSV** for the whole ledger. The payer can download or print their own receipt.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What if it doesn't go green?

If the transfer was wrong (different amount, wrong sender, sent before the request, wrong token), the request stays **Awaiting** and an explanation appears in **Needs review**. Nothing is ever marked paid by mistake. See [Review inbox](/concepts/review-inbox) and [Troubleshooting](/troubleshooting).
