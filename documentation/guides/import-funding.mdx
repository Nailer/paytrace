title: "Import a funding receipt"
description: "Save verified evidence of a past transfer to your receiving wallet, separate from customer payments."
icon: "download"
---

Use this for faucet top-ups, transfers between your own wallets, or any transfer that predates a request. See [Historical funding](/concepts/historical-funding) for the concept.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Funding receipts">In the sidebar, click **Funding receipts**, then **Import receipt** (or **Import a transfer** on the empty state).</Step>
  <Step title="Fill the form">
    - **Receipt label**: 1–120 chars, e.g. `Circle faucet funding`
    - **Transaction hash**: `0x` + 64 hex
    - **Expected amount in test USDC**: the exact amount received
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">PayTrace verifies the transfer to your **current receiving wallet** and, if `verified`, claims it and saves it.</Step>
</Steps>

## Common failures

| Message | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| The recipient matches, but the transferred amount differs… | Enter the exact amount (check decimals) |
| No positive transfer from the supported USDC contract to this recipient… | Wrong recipient wallet or a non-Circle token |
| Multiple incoming USDC events need manual review… | The transaction has several transfers to you; it can't be imported automatically |
| This transfer is already recorded. It cannot be counted twice. | Already imported, or already used to pay a request |
| The receiving wallet changed. Verify the transfer again. | Your receiving wallet was changed during import; retry |
| …not yet within the provider's finalized range. | Wait a few seconds and retry |

<Note>
Funding imports do not create review items. Each funding row links to the transaction on Monadscan (**Verified ↗**).
</Note>
